Safe by design

Everything runs inside your tenancy: the app, the workers, and the AI calls to OCI Generative AI. Nothing leaves your account. Each sandbox has a lifetime and a budget. Users sign in to the app and need no OCI account.

Install in 15 minutes

A tenancy administrator on a Pay-As-You-Go or paid account clicks Deploy to Oracle Cloud, picks a compartment, a prefix and a budget, and applies. Read the prerequisites first.
